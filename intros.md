1 Daniil Lisus is a graduating PhD candidate at the University of Toronto. 
His research targets improving the robustness of autonomous vehicles in inclement weather and geometrically degenerate conditions through the unique benefits of radar sensing.
He is also interested in combining learning methods with traditional mathematical algorithms. 

Today, he will present his work, "Beyond the peaks: Leveraging the entire imaging radar return signal for robust autonomous vehicle localization".

2 Dr. Jianzhu Huai is an Associate Researcher at the State Key Laboratory of Surveying, Mapping and Remote Sensing, Wuhan University. 
His research focuses on multi-sensor state estimation and scene modeling in complex environments, 
with particular emphasis on RGB cameras, LiDAR, and millimeter-wave radar for 3D mapping and robotic navigation.

Today, he will present his work, "Bundle Adjustment for 4D Radar Mapping".

3 Hang Zhen, a bachelor's graduate from the School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering, Wuhan University,
will pursue his master's degree at the same university under the supervision of Professor Zhi Gao.
His research focuses on SLAM, 3D/4D reconstruction, and multi-sensor fusion. 

Today, he will present his work, "Heuristic-Guided Extrinsic Calibration for 4D Radar-Camera Systems Using Dynamic Objects".

4 Liang Hu is currently a professor in the School of Intelligence Science and Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, China. 
He was an assistant professor at University of Essex, UK before joining HIT in 2022.
His research interests include localisation, navigation, control and their applications in robotics and autonomous systems. 

Today, he will present his work, "LiDAR Teach, Radar Repeat: Robust Cross-Modal Navigation in Degenerate and Varying Environments".

5 Dr. Gong Jiangkun is an Associate Professor at the School of Future Technology, Shanghai University, specializing in low-altitude radar target recognition and intelligent sensing. 
He has pioneered a cognitive micro-Doppler radar technology framework to address complex detection challenges in low-altitude airspace, and 
successfully translating this research into practical product applications. 

Today, he will present his work, "Introduction to radar Automatic Target Recognition for Advancing the Low-Altitude Economy(LAE) and Urban Air Mobility(UAM)".

6 Liu Yi, a master's student at the National University of Singapore, 
currently works on research projects related to robotic navigation. 

Today, she will present his work, "Deep Point Matching for 4D Radar Odometry".

7 Chen Yiwen is with the State Key Laboratory of Information Engineering in Surveying, Mapping, and Remote Sensing, Wuhan University. 
Her research focuses on point-cloud perception, localization, and mapping. 

Today, she will present her work, "LiDAR–Radar–IMU Fusion for Multi-Robot SLAM in Adverse Environments".

8 Binliang Wang is with the State Key Laboratory of Information Engineering in Surveying, Mapping, and Remote Sensing, Wuhan University. 
His research interests include multi-source localization and mapping for robotic systems. 

In this talk, he will introduce "Radar-Centric Sensor Fusion for Robust Indoor SLAM in Complex Environments".
