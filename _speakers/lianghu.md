Prof. Liang Hu is a Professor and PhD supervisor at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, and a recipient of China’s National High-Level Young Talent Program.

His research focuses on robotics and control, including autonomous unmanned systems, robot localization and navigation, reinforcement learning, and control.

He has published over 60 papers in leading robotics and control venues, leads multiple funded research projects, and serves on the editorial boards of major international journals and conferences.
