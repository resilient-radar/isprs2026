Dr. Gong, a Research Fellow at Wuhan University, China, specializes in the intersection of radar remote sensing, Automatic Target Recognition (ATR), cognitive radar, and machine learning.
His core work focuses on low-altitude radar systems—particularly detecting and recognizing Low-Slow-Small (LSS) targets like drones, birds, wake vortex,and vehicles—with achievements that advance China’s low-altitude economy and support the global Urban Air Mobility (UAM) industry.
